# Rick and Morty

## Overview
*Rick and Morty* is an American adult animated sci-fi sitcom created by Justin Roiland and Dan Harmon. The series follows **Rick Sanchez**, a cynical genius scientist, and his grandson **Morty Smith** as they travel across dimensions and alternate realities, often colliding with existential consequences.

# Kingsman: The Secret Service

## Overview
*Kingsman: The Secret Service* (2014) is a British spy action-comedy directed by Matthew Vaughn, based on the comic book *The Secret Service* by Mark Millar and Dave Gibbons. The film follows **Gary "Eggsy" Unwin**, a young man recruited into Kingsman, a private intelligence service, as he trains under veteran agent **Harry Hart** while a global threat emerges.
